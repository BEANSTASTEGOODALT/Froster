Froster Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Froster is a browser extension designed to provide tools for interacting with questions and tasks on DrFrost.

Data Collection

Froster does not collect, sell, or transfer personal information.

Froster accesses Website Content from DrFrost pages, such as question and task information, solely to provide its functionality. This information is processed within the extension and is used to retrieve and submit answers through DrFrost's own website services.

Froster does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information
  • Health information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication information
  • Personal communications
  • Location information
  • Web browsing history
  • User activity for tracking or analytics

Data Sharing

Froster does not sell or transfer website content or other user data to third parties. Froster does not use user data for advertising, analytics, creditworthiness, or lending purposes.

Requests made by Froster to DrFrost are made directly to DrFrost's services and are used only to provide the extension's functionality.

Data Storage

Froster does not maintain an external database or send collected website content to an external server operated by the extension developer.

The extension may use browser local storage for local settings and preferences, such as the position of the Froster interface.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Froster's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this location.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or Froster, please contact the extension developer through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
